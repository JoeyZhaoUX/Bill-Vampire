    How Much Do Subscriptions Really Cost?

    The average American spends $219 per month on subscriptions — that's $2,628 per year and $26,280 over a decade. Most people underestimate their subscription spending by 2-3x because each individual charge feels small.

    The $9.99 Illusion

    A single $9.99/month subscription seems harmless. But over 10 years, that's $1,199. If you have 5 similar subscriptions, that's nearly $6,000 — enough for a vacation, an emergency fund, or a year of car payments.

    Tips to Reduce Subscription Waste

    • Audit your subscriptions every 3 months — the ones you forgot about are the most expensive
    • Use the "cancel and see if you miss it" test — cancel anything you haven't used in 30 days
    • Watch for price increases — services regularly raise prices by $1-3/mo hoping you won't notice
    • Share family plans where possible — Netflix, Spotify, and Apple One all offer family tiers
    • Use free alternatives — many paid tools have capable free versions (Notion, Canva, etc.)
