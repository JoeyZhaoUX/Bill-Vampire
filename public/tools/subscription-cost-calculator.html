    What the total tells you

    The monthly number is useful for budgeting. The yearly number is better for comparing a subscription with an annual plan or another expense. The 10-year number shows what would happen if today's price continued for 120 months.

    A $9.99 monthly plan comes to $119.88 in one year and $1,198.80 over 10 years. The arithmetic is simple. The useful part is putting every small charge in the same list.

    Use the price you really pay

    Open the latest receipt or account page and enter the amount charged each month. For an annual plan, divide the annual price by 12 before adding it. Leave out a service you already cancelled unless you are still inside a paid period and want to compare it.

    Choose what to review first

    • Start with the highest annual total, especially if the next renewal is close.
    • Check the account that pays for each service before opening a cancellation link.
    • Save the final confirmation page and the date your access will end.

    If a charge already happened, use the refund helper to organize the receipt, account region, usage, and merchant policy before contacting support.
