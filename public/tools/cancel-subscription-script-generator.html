What makes a cancellation actually stick

  1. Cancel in the right place. App Store and Google Play subscriptions are managed in those stores. Direct subscriptions are managed in the merchant's account area.
  2. Get it in writing. Save the confirmation screen, the cancellation email, and any confirmation number.
  3. Finish the last confirmation. Continue until the account shows an end date, a cancelled status, or an option to reactivate.
  4. Watch the next billing date. Set a reminder to confirm that no further payment was taken.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel a subscription that won't let me cancel?

Identify who billed you, cancel through that store or merchant, and save the confirmation. If the account page does not work, send a written request through the merchant's published support channel.

What if they keep charging after I cancel?

Send the merchant the cancellation confirmation, charge date, and transaction identifier. If the merchant does not resolve an unrecognized or post-cancellation charge, contact your card issuer and provide the same records.
