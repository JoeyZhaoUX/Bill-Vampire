Rocket Money (formerly Truebill) works by connecting to your bank through Plaid, scanning your transaction history, and surfacing recurring charges — then offering paid concierge cancellation and bill negotiation. That model is genuinely useful if you want ongoing, account-linked oversight of every subscription you have.

But it isn't the right fit for everyone. Some people don't want a third party reading their full transaction history. Rocket Money is also US-focused, and its Premium tier uses a sliding scale (roughly $6–12/month) that some users find hard to predict. And often you don't need ongoing management at all — you need to deal with one charge, right now, without handing over your bank login.

Bill Vampire is built for that narrower moment. You paste a single charge, screenshot, or one sentence — no bank connection — and get a free cancel/refund case preview: the cancel path, the refund window, a support angle, and your next best move. If you need the full kit (exact scripts, evidence checklist, chargeback prep, reminder text), it's a one-time $4.99 — not a recurring fee.

When to use which

  • Use Rocket Money if you want a dashboard that automatically detects every recurring charge across your accounts and you're comfortable linking your bank.
  • Use a manual tracker (Bobby, Subby) if you just want reminders for subscriptions you already know about.
  • Use Bill Vampire if you have a specific surprise charge, an annual renewal you didn't want, or a subscription that's hard to cancel — and you want to cancel it or get a refund without connecting your bank.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Rocket Money alternative that doesn't link to your bank account?

Yes. Manual trackers like Bobby keep subscriptions you already know about organized, and Bill Vampire handles a specific charge you want to cancel or get refunded — both work without any bank connection.

Why would I avoid connecting my bank?

Bank-aggregator apps read your transaction history continuously through a third party. If you only need to deal with one charge, that's far more data exposure than the task requires.

Does Bill Vampire negotiate bills like Rocket Money?

No. Bill Vampire focuses on cancelling and getting refunds on specific charges, with the exact scripts and evidence you need to do it yourself — no percentage-of-savings fee.
