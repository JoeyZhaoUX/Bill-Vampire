Frequently asked questions

Can I get a refund after a free trial converted to a paid plan?

A refund may be possible. The merchant's policy, your account region, the charge date, and use after the trial all matter. Cancel the renewal, save the receipt, and use the merchant's published refund route.

What strengthens a free-trial refund request?

Keep the receipt, trial signup email, cancellation confirmation, and a short account of any use after the charge. Send the request promptly and describe only what happened.
