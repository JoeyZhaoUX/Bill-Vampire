Case Study: Microsoft 365 Refunded

Getting Refunded for a $99.99 Microsoft 365 Accidental Renewal

Emma's annual Microsoft 365 Personal subscription auto-renewed for $99.99. Read on to see how she utilized Microsoft's 30-day cancellation refund policy to get her money back.

The Surprise Annual Bill

Emma was charged $99.99 for her annual Microsoft Office 365 plan. Having switched to free alternatives, she had intended to cancel but missed the subscription renewal date.

Step 1: Open the Services Portal

Emma went to the Microsoft account services page (account.microsoft.com/services), logged in with her account email, and located her Active Microsoft 365 subscription.

Step 2: Cancel within the 30-day Window

Under the billing management options, she clicked 'Manage' → 'Cancel subscription'. Microsoft's official policy allows automatic full refunds if canceled within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date.

Step 3: Confirm Automatic Refund

During the cancel flow, the system prompted Emma with two choices: turn off recurring billing, or cancel immediately and get a refund of $99.99. She chose immediate cancellation with refund, and the money was returned to her card within 5 days.

$99.99 Refund of $99.99 secured

Microsoft has a very clear 30-day automatic refund policy for annual renewals. The key is clicking the correct immediate cancellation button rather than simply turning off recurring billing.
