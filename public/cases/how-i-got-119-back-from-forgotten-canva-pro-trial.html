Canva Pro $119.88 Trial Renewal: Worked Refund Request Example

This educational scenario shows how someone charged $119.88 after a Canva Pro trial could organize a prompt refund request. It is not a verified customer story or a promise that Canva will approve a refund.

The Example Charge

Assume a Canva Pro trial converts to an annual plan and a $119.88 transaction appears on the card. First confirm the billing account, transaction date, and payment channel.

Step 1: Instantly Cancel

Open Canva's Billing & plans settings and stop future renewal. Save the confirmation; cancellation usually prevents another charge but does not itself guarantee a refund of the current one.

Step 2: Collect Evidence

Save the receipt, billing-page screenshot, cancellation confirmation, and a short timeline. Note whether the subscription was used after the trial converted.

Step 3: Request Review

Use Canva's official support path to make a concise goodwill request with the transaction details and evidence. Canva decides eligibility and timing under the terms that apply to the account.

$119.88 Example transaction amount

Acting promptly and keeping clear evidence can support a request, but neither the merchant nor a card issuer is guaranteed to return the charge.
