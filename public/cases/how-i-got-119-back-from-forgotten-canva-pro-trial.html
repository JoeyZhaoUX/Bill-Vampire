Case Study: Canva Pro Refund

How I Got $119.88 Back From a Forgotten Canva Pro Trial

Sarah was charged $119.88 after missing her Canva Pro annual billing reminder. Read the exact refund request workflow she used to get the charge fully reversed in under 24 hours.

The Surprise Charge

Sarah signed up for a 30-day Canva Pro trial and forgot to set a calendar reminder. Exactly 30 days later, she woke up to an unwanted annual renewal transaction of $119.88 on her credit card.

Step 1: Instantly Cancel

Sarah didn't wait. She immediately logged into Canva, went to Account Settings → Billing & plans, and canceled the future renewal so no further fees could apply.

Step 2: Collect Evidence

She took screenshots of her billing tab showing the cancellation confirmation and saved her transaction receipt number as primary proof of good faith.

Step 3: Submit the Script

Sarah used Bill Vampire's customized refund helper to draft an email asking for a goodwill refund. Since she hadn't used the account since the trial expired, Canva support approved the refund within hours.

$119.88 Recovered in full

Sarah's case shows that card issuers and merchants are highly likely to refund accidental renewals if the cancellation is made immediately.
