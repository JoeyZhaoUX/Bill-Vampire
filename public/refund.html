Refund Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1. Free Tier

Bill Vampire's core features are free to use and require no payment. No refund applies to the free tier.

2. Pro Upgrade (19 USD One-Time · 12 USD Founding)

The Pro upgrade is a one-time digital purchase processed through Creem. Because of the immediate nature of digital goods delivery, we generally do not offer refunds after purchase.

However, we want you to be satisfied. If you experience any of the following, we will issue a full refund:

  • You were charged but Pro features did not activate
  • You were charged multiple times for the same purchase
  • You request a refund within 3 days of purchase and have not extensively used Pro features

3. Patrol Subscription (4.99 USD / month · 39 USD / year)

Patrol is billed recurring through Creem. You can cancel at any time from your Creem receipt or by emailing us; once cancelled we stop all future charges at the end of the current billing period. We do not pro-rate refunds for partial months already elapsed, with two exceptions:

  • You were charged for a renewal but Patrol had already been uninstalled before the renewal date — we refund that renewal in full.
  • You were charged more than once in the same billing period due to a processing error.

4. Tips

Tips are voluntary contributions to support the development of Bill Vampire. Tips are non-refundable, as they are not tied to any product or service delivery.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at hello@billvampire.com with your purchase receipt or Creem order number. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Creem and will be returned to your original payment method. Processing time depends on your payment provider, typically 5-10 business days.
