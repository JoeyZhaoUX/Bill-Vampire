How to Cancel Hulu — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2-3 minutes easy Last Updated: 2026-05-21 $17.99/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Canceling Hulu is quick and can be done entirely online. Your account stays active through the end of your current billing period, so you won't lose access immediately.

Official account or cancellation path: Hulu

Go to your Hulu Account page

Visit secure.hulu.com/account in your browser and sign in. You can also access this from the Hulu app by tapping your profile → Account.

Click "Cancel" under Your Subscription

In the Your Subscription section, click "Cancel". If you have the Disney Bundle, you'll see options to manage individual services.

If you're on a Hulu + Live TV plan, consider switching to the basic Hulu plan ($7.99/mo) first — you might only need to drop Live TV.

Decline the retention offer

Hulu may offer you a discounted rate, a plan switch, or a pause option. Click "Continue to Cancel" if you want to proceed with full cancellation.

Hulu's pause feature (up to 12 weeks) is genuinely useful if you're on the fence. You won't be charged during the pause.

Confirm your cancellation

Click "Cancel Subscription" on the final confirmation screen. You'll see your last date of access. An email confirmation will be sent to your registered email.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you subscribed through Apple, Roku, or Amazon, you must cancel through their billing systems — not on Hulu's website.
  • If you have the Disney Bundle through Hulu, canceling Hulu cancels the entire bundle including Disney+ and ESPN+.
  • Any add-ons (HBO Max, Showtime, etc.) purchased through Hulu will also be canceled.
  • Your profile, watchlist, and viewing history are preserved if you re-subscribe within a reasonable timeframe.

✗ Refund policy

Hulu does not offer prorated refunds for unused portions of your billing cycle. Your access continues through the end of your current period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund from Hulu?
Hulu does not typically offer refunds for partial billing periods. If you were double-charged or experienced a billing error, contact Hulu support for resolution.
Will I lose my Hulu profiles if I cancel?
Hulu retains your profile data, viewing history, and preferences for some time after cancellation. If you re-subscribe within a few months, everything should be restored.
Can I cancel Hulu but keep Disney+?
Yes. If you have the Disney Bundle through Hulu, you can switch to Disney+ only. Go to Account → Manage Add-ons and adjust your bundle.
What happens to my Hulu + Live TV DVR recordings?
All cloud DVR recordings are deleted when your Live TV subscription ends. There's no way to save them externally.
