How to Cancel ChatGPT Plus — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 1-2 minutes easy Last Updated: 2026-05-21 $20/mo 🛡️ Written & Verified by Consumer Advocacy Team

Canceling ChatGPT Plus is quick and painless — no retention calls, no dark patterns. OpenAI lets you cancel with 3 clicks and you keep access through the end of your paid period.

Open ChatGPT Settings

Go to chat.openai.com and click your profile icon in the bottom-left corner. Select "Settings" from the menu. Alternatively, navigate directly to chat.openai.com/settings/subscription.

Click "Manage my subscription"

In the Settings panel, find the "Subscription" section and click "Manage my subscription". This opens the billing portal.

If you subscribed through the iOS app, you'll see a note directing you to Apple's subscription settings instead.

Click "Cancel plan"

In the billing portal, click "Cancel plan" or "Cancel subscription". You may be shown a comparison of Plus vs Free features.

Confirm cancellation

Click "Cancel subscription" on the confirmation dialog. You'll receive an email confirmation. Your Plus access continues until the end of your current billing cycle.

Note your billing date — you can keep using GPT-4, DALL-E, and other Plus features until that date.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you subscribed via Apple App Store (iOS app), you MUST cancel through Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device. Canceling in ChatGPT settings won't stop Apple billing.
  • After cancellation, you lose access to GPT-4, DALL-E 3, Advanced Data Analysis, custom GPTs, and priority access during peak times.
  • Your chat history is preserved on the free plan — you won't lose past conversations.
  • If you have a ChatGPT Team or Enterprise plan, only the workspace admin can cancel.

✓ Refund may be possible

OpenAI doesn't advertise refunds, but if you contact support at help.openai.com within a few days of a charge you didn't intend (e.g., forgot to cancel before renewal), they often issue a one-time courtesy refund.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my chat history if I cancel ChatGPT Plus?
No. All your conversations, custom GPTs, and settings are preserved. You simply lose access to Plus-only features like GPT-4 and DALL-E.
Can I get a refund for ChatGPT Plus?
OpenAI doesn't offer automatic refunds, but you can request one by contacting help.openai.com within a few days of being charged. They've been known to issue credits for unused portions.
What's the difference between ChatGPT Free and Plus?
Free gives you access to GPT-3.5 with usage limits. Plus ($20/mo) gives GPT-4, DALL-E 3, web browsing, code interpreter, custom GPTs, and priority access.
How do I cancel ChatGPT if I subscribed through Apple?
On iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → ChatGPT → Cancel. On Mac: App Store → Account → Subscriptions → ChatGPT → Cancel.
