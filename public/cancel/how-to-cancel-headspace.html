How to Cancel Headspace on Web, iPhone, or Android (2026)

⏰ 3 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-08-30 $12.99/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Headspace does not have one universal cancel button. The correct route depends on who took the payment: Headspace, Apple, or Google Play. Find the receipt first, because cancelling in the wrong place leaves the real subscription untouched.

Official account or cancellation path Headspace

Identify the billing provider

Check the latest receipt or the subscription list on your phone. If Headspace appears in Apple Settings or Google Play, use that store. If neither store lists it, sign in to Headspace in a browser.

Do this before opening a support request. The store that collected the payment normally controls cancellation and refund decisions.

For a direct Headspace subscription, use the manage page

Sign in at headspace.com from a desktop or mobile browser, open headspace.com/subscription/manage, and choose Cancel Membership. If the button is missing, Headspace directs users to help@headspace.com.

The current help page says to use a browser, not the mobile app, for a subscription purchased directly from Headspace.

For Apple or Google Play, cancel in the store

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, choose your name, Subscriptions, Headspace, and Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play, choose Subscriptions, Headspace, and Cancel subscription.

Cancelling a store-billed plan on headspace.com does not stop the store renewal.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

Verify the end date

Return to the same subscription screen and confirm that renewal is off or an expiration date is shown. Save a screenshot or confirmation email and check the next statement.

If you received Headspace through an organization, the official help page says that access expires according to that agreement and does not need to be cancelled.

⚠️ Watch out

  • A direct Headspace subscription, an Apple subscription, and a Google Play subscription have different cancellation routes.
  • Headspace's current help page describes direct subscriptions as non-refundable, while allowing users with special circumstances to ask help@headspace.com for an individual review.
  • The same help page describes Apple and Google Play purchases as final and non-refundable, subject to each store's payment policy.
  • A Headspace Mental Health Coaching subscription has a separate cancellation process.

✗ Refund policy

Headspace's current help page says direct subscriptions are non-refundable, though special circumstances may be reviewed individually at help@headspace.com. Apple and Google purchases are subject to the relevant store policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can I not see Headspace in my iPhone subscriptions?
The plan may have been purchased directly from Headspace, through Google Play, or under a different Apple ID. Match the account and provider to the receipt before trying another cancel path.
Can I get a Headspace refund after renewal?
Headspace describes direct subscriptions as non-refundable but says special circumstances can be reviewed by its support team. Apple and Google purchases follow the store's payment and refund process.
What if the Cancel Membership button is missing?
First verify that the account email matches the receipt. For a direct purchase, Headspace says to email help@headspace.com if the button is not available.
Do employer-provided Headspace plans need cancellation?
Headspace says a free subscription provided by an organization expires according to the agreement. Check the benefit terms before buying a separate plan.
