How to Cancel Bumble — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 1-2 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $16.99/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Like all dating apps, Bumble subscriptions are managed through Apple or Google — not the app itself. Deleting Bumble won't stop charges. Here's how to properly cancel.

Official account or cancellation path: Bumble

Don't just delete the app

Uninstalling Bumble does not cancel your subscription. Billing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

Cancel through your app store

iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Bumble → Cancel. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bumble → Cancel.

Confirm cancellation

Tap "Confirm" to cancel. Premium features remain active until the end of your billing period.

Free Bumble still lets you match and message — you just lose SuperSwipes, Spotlight, Travel mode, and unlimited extends.

Optionally snooze or delete your profile

If you want a break without deleting: use Bumble's Snooze Mode. To fully remove your profile: Settings → Delete Account in the app.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Deleting the Bumble app does NOT cancel your subscription.
  • If you subscribed through bumble.com (web), manage it at bumble.com/en/help.
  • Free Bumble still works for basic matching and messaging.
  • Bumble BFF and Bizz features are separate from dating subscriptions.

✓ Refund may be possible

Refunds through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play. Bumble direct refunds are rare — use your app store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will deleting Bumble cancel my subscription?
No. You must cancel through Apple Settings or Google Play Store. The app cannot stop its own billing.
Can I get a refund from Bumble?
Request refunds through Apple or Google's platforms. Bumble's own refund policy is strict, but Apple/Google may grant refunds for recent charges.
What's free on Bumble?
Free Bumble includes basic swiping, matching, and messaging. Premium adds SuperSwipes, unlimited extends, travel mode, advanced filters, and seeing who liked you.
What's Bumble Snooze Mode?
Snooze Mode hides your profile temporarily (24 hours to indefinitely) without deleting your account or matches. Useful if you want a break.
