How to Cancel Crunchyroll — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2 minutes easy Verified 2026-05-22 $7.99/mo

Crunchyroll has a free ad-supported tier, so canceling just means downgrading. You keep access to a limited library with ads — just not the full premium catalog or offline downloads.

Go to Crunchyroll account settings

Visit crunchyroll.com/account/subscription while signed in. Or: click your profile → Settings → Subscription.

Click "Cancel Subscription"

Find the cancellation option in your subscription settings and click it.

Confirm cancellation

Confirm your choice. Premium features remain until the end of your billing period, then you switch to the free tier.

The free tier gives you access to a limited anime library with ads. New episodes may have a 1-week delay compared to Premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I still watch anime free on Crunchyroll?
Yes. The free tier includes a limited anime library with ads. Most popular shows are available, but new episodes may have a 1-week delay vs. Premium.
Can I get a refund from Crunchyroll?
Crunchyroll may offer refunds within a few days of charge. Contact support through their help center. Apple/Google subscriptions go through their refund processes.
What's a free alternative to Crunchyroll?
Crunchyroll's own free tier (ad-supported), YouTube (some anime channels), and Tubi (limited anime library, free with ads) are options.
