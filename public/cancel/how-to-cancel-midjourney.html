How to Cancel Midjourney — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $10/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Midjourney subscriptions are managed through their website at midjourney.com/account. You keep access to your generated images after canceling, but lose the ability to create new ones.

Official account or cancellation path: Midjourney

Go to your Midjourney account page

Visit midjourney.com/account and sign in with your Discord account. You'll see your current plan and billing details.

Click "Manage Sub" or find subscription settings

Look for "Manage Sub" or "Manage Plan" button on your account page. This opens your subscription management panel.

You can also type /subscribe in any Midjourney Discord channel to get a link to your account page.

Click "Cancel Plan"

Click "Cancel Plan" in your subscription settings. Midjourney will show your current billing cycle end date.

Confirm cancellation

Confirm your cancellation. Your remaining image generation credits are available until the end of your billing period. After that, you can still view and download your previously generated images.

Use your remaining monthly credits before the period ends — they don't carry over or get refunded.

⚠️ Watch out

  • You keep access to all previously generated images in your gallery at midjourney.com — they're not deleted.
  • Unused generation credits for the current period are forfeited at cancellation — use them first.
  • If you subscribed through the yearly plan, you may not get a refund for unused months.
  • You retain commercial usage rights for images generated while you had a paid subscription.

✓ Refund may be possible

Midjourney offers refunds if you've used less than 1% of your monthly GPU allocation. For annual plans, partial refunds may be available. Contact support through their Discord server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my Midjourney images if I cancel?
No. All images you generated remain in your gallery at midjourney.com and can be downloaded anytime. You just can't generate new ones.
Can I get a refund from Midjourney?
Midjourney offers refunds if you've used less than 1% of your monthly GPU time (about 2-3 image generations). Contact their support through Discord for refund requests.
Do I keep commercial rights to my images after canceling?
Yes. Images generated while you had a paid subscription retain their commercial usage rights even after cancellation. Free trial images have more restrictive terms.
What's a free alternative to Midjourney?
DALL-E (limited free generations via Bing Image Creator), Stable Diffusion (free and open source, runs locally), and Leonardo AI (free tier with daily credits) are popular alternatives.
