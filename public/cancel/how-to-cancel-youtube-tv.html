How to Cancel YouTube TV & Get a Refund

⏰ 2 minutes easy Last Updated: 2026-06-11 $72.99/mo 🛡️ Written & Verified by Consumer Advocacy Team

YouTube TV has a simple subscription model with no cancellation fees, but billing automatically rolls over unless canceled. You can cancel your subscription or free trial at any time.

Open YouTube TV settings

Go to tv.youtube.com on your computer or mobile web browser. Click on your profile photo in the top right and select "Settings". Note: iOS users cannot cancel subscriptions from the YouTube TV App.

Go to Membership section

In the Settings menu, click on "Membership". You will see your active membership, Base Plan, and any active Add-ons.

Click Pause or Cancel Membership

Next to your Base Plan, click "Manage". Then click "Pause or cancel membership" or "Cancel membership".

YouTube TV allows you to pause your membership for up to 6 months if you just want a temporary break.

Choose Cancellation and Confirm

Click "Cancel" to proceed. Select your reason for leaving, then click "Continue canceling". Finally, click "Yes, cancel" to complete the process.

If you are on a paid plan, your service remains active until the end of your billing cycle. If on a free trial, your service ends immediately.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Free trial cancellations take effect IMMEDIATELY. You will lose access to all channels, DVR recordings, and add-ons right away.
  • Paid plans remain active until your next billing date — no partial or prorated refunds are standard.
  • If you billed through Apple/iTunes, you must cancel through iOS Settings -> Apple ID Subscriptions.

✓ Refund may be possible

Refunds are generally not provided. However, Google provides an automatic refund tool for certain situations (e.g., accidental renewals or unrecognized charges) at support.google.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund for YouTube TV?
YouTube TV does not offer refunds for partial billing periods. However, if you forgot to cancel a trial or had an accidental charge, you can request a refund through the Google Play Refund Support page.
What happens to my recorded DVR shows when I cancel?
Your recorded shows in your Library are saved for 21 days. If you re-subscribe within 21 days, your recordings will be restored. After that, they are permanently deleted.
