How to Cancel Grammarly and Stop the Next Renewal (2026)

⏰ 3 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-08-30 $12/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Cancelling Grammarly stops automatic renewal while paid access continues through the period you already bought. If a charge has already posted, a refund is a separate request. Grammarly's current support page says refunds are issued only when required by law, so do not rely on older claims of a general seven-day guarantee.

Official account or cancellation path Grammarly

Sign in to the account that is being billed

Open Grammarly's Account Hub in a browser. Choose Account in the left sidebar, then open Your account and Subscription.

If the cancel button is missing, first check the email address on the receipt. Grammarly says this often means you are signed in to a different account.

Choose Cancel Subscription

Scroll to the bottom of the Subscription page and choose Cancel Subscription. In the next window, choose Continue.

On mobile, use account.grammarly.com in the browser. An App Store purchase must be cancelled through Apple instead.

Choose a reason and confirm

Choose a cancellation reason, then select Cancel Subscription. Do not stop at the reason screen; the account should show that renewal is no longer scheduled.

A survey or alternative plan is not a cancellation confirmation. Save the screen that shows the subscription will end.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

Keep the confirmation and paid-through date

Save the final screen or email and note the end date. Grammarly says cancelling stops automatic renewal while paid features remain available through the current subscription period.

If the charge came through Apple, keep the Apple receipt as well; Apple handles that refund route.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Grammarly's current refund page does not advertise a general seven-day money-back guarantee. It says refunds are issued only when required by law.
  • An App Store purchase must be cancelled and, if needed, refunded through Apple.
  • Deleting a Grammarly account is a different action from cancelling a subscription.
  • For a Superhuman user, Grammarly says cancelling through the associated account applies across the other applications in the suite.

✗ Refund policy

Grammarly's current published policy says refunds are issued only when required by law. For an App Store purchase, contact Apple. Cancel the renewal first, then use Grammarly's official refund page to decide whether a request is appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Grammarly stop working the moment I cancel?
No. Grammarly's current support page says paid features remain available for the rest of the subscription period you already paid for.
Can I get a Grammarly refund after renewal?
Grammarly's published rule says refunds are issued only when required by law. If special circumstances apply, describe the charge date, account email, and why you believe the request qualifies; do not rely on an unofficial seven-day promise.
Why is there no cancel button?
Check that the signed-in account matches the billed email. If you cannot access the paid account, use Grammarly Support. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple.
Do I need to delete my Grammarly account?
No. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Account deletion is separate and removes account data, so do not use it as a substitute for confirming cancellation.
