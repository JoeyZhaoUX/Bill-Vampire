How to Cancel Calm — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $14.99/mo 🛡️ Written & Verified by Consumer Advocacy Team

Calm cancellation works the same way as most app subscriptions — through Apple, Google, or their website depending on where you signed up. Some free content remains available after canceling.

Check where you subscribed

Most Calm subscriptions are through Apple App Store or Google Play. Check Settings → Subscriptions on your device first.

Cancel through the correct platform

Apple: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Calm → Cancel. Google: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Calm → Cancel. Web: calm.com/account/subscription → Cancel.

If you can't find Calm in your subscriptions, try the other platform or check calm.com/account.

Confirm cancellation

Tap "Confirm" to finalize. Your Premium access continues until your billing period ends.

Verify in the app

Open the Calm app and check your account settings to confirm the subscription shows as canceled. Some free content (Daily Calm, limited sleep stories) remains accessible.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Lifetime subscriptions cannot be canceled (one-time purchase — you keep it forever).
  • Annual plans are not refundable after the first 30 days in most cases.
  • Canceling on calm.com does NOT cancel Apple/Google billing — cancel through the platform you subscribed on.
  • Downloaded content for offline use becomes unavailable after cancellation.

✓ Refund may be possible

Calm offers refunds within 30 days of annual purchase through their support. Apple/Google subscriptions must be refunded through their respective platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Calm have free content after canceling?
Yes. Calm's free tier includes limited meditation content, some sleep stories, and the Daily Calm (new daily meditation). Most of the library requires Premium.
Can I get a refund from Calm?
Annual subscriptions may be refundable within 30 days. For Apple/Google, use their refund processes. For direct subscriptions, contact Calm support.
What's the Calm Lifetime plan?
Calm offers a lifetime subscription (one-time payment of ~$400) that never expires. If you purchased this, you don't need to cancel anything.
What's a free alternative to Calm?
Insight Timer (100k+ free meditations), Smiling Mind (completely free), UCLA Mindful (research-backed, free), and YouTube meditation channels.
