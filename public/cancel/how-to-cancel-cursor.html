Cursor subscriptions are sold directly through Cursor. Open the dashboard, find Billing, and cancel renewal there. Your projects and settings remain; the current Hobby plan has limited Agent requests and limited Tab completions, while exact usage limits may change.

Open Cursor settings

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in Cursor's free plan?
Cursor currently lists Hobby as free with limited Agent requests and limited Tab completions. Pro adds extended Agent limits and access to frontier models. Exact usage is shown in Cursor's live pricing and dashboard.
Can I get a refund from Cursor?
Contact Cursor at hi@cursor.com with the account email, charge date, amount, and reason. Cursor does not publish a universal refund promise, so eligibility is reviewed case by case.
Will I lose my settings and extensions?
No. All your Cursor settings, keybindings, extensions, and project configurations are preserved regardless of plan.
