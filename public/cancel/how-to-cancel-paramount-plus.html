How to Cancel Paramount+ — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2 minutes easy Verified 2026-05-22 $11.99/mo

Paramount+ cancellation is done through their website and takes about 2 minutes. You keep access through the end of your billing period.

Go to your Paramount+ account

Visit paramountplus.com and sign in. Click your profile icon in the top-right and select "Account".

Find subscription settings

On the Account page, locate "My Subscription" section showing your current plan and renewal date.

If subscribed through Apple, Amazon, or Roku, you'll see a note directing you to cancel through that platform.

Click "Cancel Subscription"

Click "Cancel Subscription". Paramount+ will show what you'll lose and your access end date.

Confirm cancellation

Click through any retention offers and confirm. You'll receive an email confirmation and keep access until your billing period ends.

Paramount+ often runs $1.99/mo deals for returning subscribers, so you may get a great offer if you want to come back later.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you subscribed via Apple, Amazon, or Roku, cancel through their subscription settings.
  • If you have the Paramount+ with Showtime bundle, canceling removes both services.
  • Downloaded content becomes unavailable after your subscription ends.
  • Promotional pricing won't be available if you re-subscribe at full price.

✗ Refund policy

Paramount+ does not offer prorated refunds. Access continues until the end of your current billing period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund from Paramount+?
Paramount+ does not typically offer refunds for partial periods. Contact their help center for billing errors. If billed through Apple/Google, request refund through them.
What's the difference between Paramount+ plans?
Essential ($5.99/mo) has ads and limited content. With Showtime ($11.99/mo) is ad-free, includes Showtime content, offline downloads, and local CBS live.
Will my watchlist be saved?
Yes. Your profile, watchlist, and viewing history are preserved. If you re-subscribe, everything will be as you left it.
Does Paramount+ offer a free trial?
Paramount+ occasionally offers 7-day free trials for new subscribers. Check their homepage or look for promotional offers through partner services.
