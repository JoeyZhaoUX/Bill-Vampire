How to Cancel Nintendo Switch Online — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2-3 minutes medium Verified 2026-05-22 $3.99/mo

Nintendo Switch Online cancellation simply turns off auto-renewal. Your membership stays active until the expiration date. You can do it from the Switch console or Nintendo's website.

Go to Nintendo eShop or website

On Switch: Open Nintendo eShop → click your profile icon → Nintendo Switch Online. On web: ec.nintendo.com/my/membership.

Find auto-renewal settings

Navigate to your membership settings. Look for "Turn Off Automatic Renewal" option.

Turn off auto-renewal

Click "Turn Off Automatic Renewal" and confirm. Your membership continues until the current period expires.

If you have a family plan, only the family group admin can manage the subscription.

Confirm via email

Nintendo sends a confirmation email. Your online features (multiplayer, cloud saves, classic game library) remain active until the expiration date.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Cloud save data may be deleted after a period without an active subscription — download important saves.
  • Online multiplayer stops working after your membership expires.
  • NES/SNES/N64/Game Boy games (included with membership) become inaccessible.
  • Family plan members all lose access if the admin cancels.
  • The Expansion Pack (N64, Sega Genesis games) is part of the higher tier — it's removed with cancellation.

✗ Refund policy

Nintendo rarely offers refunds for Switch Online memberships. Contact Nintendo support to request — success varies by region and timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play online without Nintendo Switch Online?
Most games require NSO for online multiplayer. Free-to-play games (Fortnite, Apex, Rocket League) do NOT require NSO.
What happens to my cloud saves?
Cloud saves are preserved for a period (around 6 months) after membership expires. After that, they may be deleted. Download saves locally as backup.
Can I get a refund from Nintendo?
Nintendo's refund policy is strict. Contact Nintendo customer support for requests within a few days of purchase. They rarely offer refunds after the membership has been active.
Is the Expansion Pack worth keeping?
The base NSO ($3.99/mo or $19.99/year) gets you cloud saves and basic online. Expansion Pack ($49.99/year) adds N64, Sega, and GBA games. Cancel Expansion Pack and keep base to save money.
