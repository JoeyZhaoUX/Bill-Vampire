How to Cancel Nintendo Switch Online Before It Renews (2026)

⏰ 3 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-08-25 Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Nintendo treats cancellation as turning off automatic renewal. The membership keeps working until its expiration date, but Nintendo says automatic renewal must be stopped at least 48 hours before that date.

Official account or cancellation path Nintendo Switch Online

Confirm which Nintendo Account bought the plan

Check the membership receipt and sign in with the Nintendo Account that purchased the individual or family membership. Other family-group accounts cannot change that payment.

Do this before the final two days. Nintendo's US support page says automatic renewal must be turned off at least 48 hours before expiration.

Turn off renewal on the Switch console

Open Nintendo eShop with the purchasing account. Select the user icon in the upper-right corner, open Account Information, choose Nintendo Switch Online, and turn off automatic renewal.

The membership remains active until the expiration date shown in the account.

Or cancel from the Nintendo Account website

Sign in at accounts.nintendo.com/portal. Open Nintendo Switch Online, choose the active plan, select Cancel pass by turning off automatic renewal, and confirm.

If no plan appears, check whether the receipt belongs to another Nintendo Account.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

Record the expiration date

Make sure the account shows automatic renewal as off and save the confirmation. Check any online play, cloud backup, or game-library feature you rely on before the membership expires.

Turning off renewal stops the next payment. It does not shorten the time already paid for.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Nintendo says automatic renewal must be stopped at least 48 hours before the membership expires.
  • The current US support policy does not provide refunds or credits for time remaining after cancellation.
  • Save Data Cloud backup stops after expiration. Nintendo says a new membership started within 180 days can restore access to the cloud backups, while saves stored on the console remain available.
  • When a family membership ends, the other members of the family group also lose the service.
  • A seven-day free trial changes to a paid membership unless automatic renewal is stopped before the trial ends.
  • For a family membership, use the Nintendo Account that purchased the plan.

✗ Refund policy

Nintendo's current US support page says it does not provide refunds or credits for remaining membership time. If the charge was unauthorized or local law applies, contact Nintendo support with the receipt and account details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Nintendo Switch Online stop immediately when I cancel?
No. Nintendo says the membership remains active through its expiration date after automatic renewal is turned off.
Can I get a refund for unused Nintendo Switch Online time?
Nintendo's current US cancellation page says no refund or credit is provided for the remaining time. Local law may provide additional rights in some regions.
How do I stop a seven-day free trial from charging me?
Turn off automatic renewal before the trial ends. Nintendo says the trial otherwise converts to the individual one-month membership selected at signup.
Why can I not turn off renewal from my account?
The plan may have been purchased by a different Nintendo Account, including another member of a family group. Match the account to the receipt.
