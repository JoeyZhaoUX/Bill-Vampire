How to Cancel FuboTV & Avoid Free Trial Auto-Renewal Charges

⏰ 3-5 minutes hard Last Updated: 2026-06-11 $79.99/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

FuboTV subscriptions automatically renew at the end of your billing cycle or free trial. Canceling your FuboTV free trial will terminate the service immediately, so time it carefully to avoid a sudden charge of $79.99 or more.

Official account or cancellation path FuboTV

Sign in to Fubo.tv

Go to fubo.tv on a web browser or mobile browser. Sign in using your registered email and password. Note: You cannot cancel FuboTV through most TV apps directly.

Navigate to My Account

Hover or tap on your profile icon in the top right corner and select "My Account" from the dropdown menu.

Make sure you are logged into the primary profile of your subscription.

Go to Subscription and Billing

Locate the "Subscription & Billing" section. Here, you will see your current plan, renewal date, and next billing amount.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

Check my FuboTV refund options →

Click Cancel Subscription

Scroll down and click the "Cancel Subscription" button. Fubo will show several retention screens or discount offers.

Do not select 'Pause Subscription' if you want to completely stop billing. Choose 'Complete Cancellation' or 'Cancel Subscription' again.

Confirm and check email

Provide a reason for canceling if prompted, then click "Cancel Subscription" to confirm. Check your email for a confirmation receipt containing your cancellation reference.

If you are on a free trial, canceling will immediately cut off your streaming access.

⚠️ Watch out

  • FuboTV free trials terminate IMMEDIATELY upon cancellation. If you cancel on Day 1 of a 7-day trial, you lose access instantly.
  • FuboTV does not offer prorated refunds for mid-cycle cancellations of paid plans.
  • If you subscribed through a third party like Apple, Roku, or Amazon, you must cancel through their respective subscription platforms.
  • Be careful of 'Pause' offers, which will resume billing automatically after a set period.

✓ Refund may be possible

FuboTV states all fees are non-refundable. However, in cases of unauthorized charges or immediate trial expiration issues, support may grant exceptions if disputed promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I get a refund if I cancel FuboTV after being charged?
FuboTV has a strict no-refund policy for paid subscriptions once charged. However, if you were accidentally billed due to a trial transition or system error, you can submit a billing dispute.
Can I cancel FuboTV on Apple TV or Roku?
Yes, if you billed through Apple or Roku, go to your Apple ID Subscriptions or Roku Account Portal to cancel. Fubo cannot process cancellations or refunds for third-party billing.
What happens if I pause my Fubo subscription?
Pausing suspends billing for up to 3 months, keeping your profile and DVR settings. After the pause period ends, your credit card will be billed automatically at the standard rate.
