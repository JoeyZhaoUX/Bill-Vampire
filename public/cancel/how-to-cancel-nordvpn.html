How to Cancel NordVPN — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 3-5 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-05-21 $12.99/mo 🛡️ Written & Verified by Consumer Advocacy Team

NordVPN has a 30-day money-back guarantee, so if you're within that window you can get a full refund. Even after 30 days, you can stop auto-renewal so you won't be charged again when your plan expires.

Log in to your Nord Account

Go to my.nordaccount.com and sign in with your NordVPN credentials. Click on "Services" or navigate to the NordVPN section.

Go to Subscription settings

Find your NordVPN subscription and click "Change plan" or navigate to the billing/subscription section. Look for "Auto-renewal" settings.

Turn off auto-renewal

Click "Cancel automatic payments" or toggle off auto-renewal. This prevents future charges. Your VPN access continues until your current plan expires.

If you're within 30 days of purchase, skip this step and go straight to support for a full refund instead.

Request a refund (if within 30 days)

If you're within the 30-day money-back period: click the chat bubble for live support, tell them you want to cancel and request a refund. They'll process it within 5-10 business days.

Be direct: 'I'd like to cancel my subscription and request a refund under the 30-day guarantee.' Don't explain too much — they're required to honor it.

Confirm cancellation via email

Check your email for a cancellation/refund confirmation from NordVPN. If paying via PayPal, the refund appears in 5-10 days. Credit card refunds take 5-10 business days.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play, you must cancel through their subscription settings and request refund through them.
  • After the 30-day window, you cannot get a refund — but you can stop auto-renewal so you keep access until expiry without being re-charged.
  • NordVPN often sells 1-year or 2-year plans. Turning off auto-renewal means you keep the full remaining time but won't be charged again.
  • Third-party purchases (StackSocial, etc.) have different refund policies — contact that vendor instead.

✓ Refund may be possible

NordVPN offers a full 30-day money-back guarantee with no questions asked. Contact live chat support to request it. After 30 days, no refund is available but you keep access through your plan expiration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund from NordVPN?
Yes, within 30 days of purchase under their money-back guarantee. Contact live chat support and request a refund. After 30 days, no refund is available but you can cancel auto-renewal.
How long does a NordVPN refund take?
Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days. PayPal refunds may be faster (2-3 days). Credit card refunds depend on your bank.
Will my NordVPN account be deleted when I cancel?
No. Your account remains active but without VPN access after your plan expires. You can reactivate anytime by purchasing a new plan.
What's a good free alternative to NordVPN?
ProtonVPN offers a free tier with unlimited data (3 server locations). Windscribe offers 10GB/month free. Built-in browser VPNs (Opera) work for basic browsing privacy.
