How to Cancel NordVPN and Check Refund Eligibility (2026)

⏰ 5 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-08-25 Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Stopping renewal and requesting a refund are two separate actions. A direct NordVPN purchase is cancelled in Nord Account. The 30-day money-back guarantee applies to an eligible first purchase, not an ordinary renewal.

Official account or cancellation path NordVPN

Check where the subscription was purchased

Open the receipt and identify NordVPN, Apple, Google Play, Amazon, or another retailer as the billing provider. The provider determines where the renewal and refund are handled.

A long-term plan may have plenty of paid time left. Turning off renewal prevents the next charge and does not create a refund for the unused months.

Turn off a direct NordVPN renewal

Sign in to Nord Account and open Billing. Select Cancel next to Auto-renewal, then choose Cancel auto-renewal. The status should change to Off.

Save the confirmation email. It is the clearest proof that the next renewal was stopped.

Use the store for a store-billed plan

Apple, Google Play, and Amazon subscriptions are cancelled through the matching store account. A retailer or reseller purchase follows that seller's terms.

Use the account shown on the receipt. A different Apple or Google account will not display the subscription.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

Request a refund only when the payment qualifies

For an eligible direct first purchase within 30 days, contact NordVPN support and use the verification link it provides. Choose the eligible payment, select Refund, provide the requested reason, and confirm the request.

A completed refund can take up to 10 business days to reach the original payment method.

⚠️ Watch out

  • NordVPN's 30-day guarantee applies to the initial subscription purchase. A normal renewal is excluded unless local law requires otherwise.
  • Apple decides refunds for Apple App Store purchases. Amazon handles Amazon Appstore purchases.
  • NordVPN says eligible Google Play payments can be requested through its support, while payments from the last 48 hours can also be requested in Google Play.
  • A retailer or reseller purchase must be taken back to that seller.

✓ Refund may be possible

An eligible initial subscription purchase can be refunded within 30 days. Renewed periods are excluded from that guarantee. Store and reseller purchases follow the billing provider's route, and local consumer law may add rights.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does NordVPN offer a refund for a renewed subscription?
Its current refund policy says the 30-day guarantee covers the initial purchase and does not cover a renewed subscription period, unless applicable law requires otherwise.
How do I know auto-renewal is off?
Nord Account should show the auto-renewal status as Off, and NordVPN says it sends a confirmation email after a successful cancellation.
How long can a NordVPN refund take?
NordVPN says its processing can take a few hours, and the completed refund may take up to 10 business days to reach the account depending on the payment method.
Can NordVPN refund an Apple purchase?
No. NordVPN's policy directs Apple App Store refund requests to Apple.
