How to Cancel Duolingo Plus (Super) — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 1-2 minutes medium Verified 2026-05-22 $6.99/mo

Duolingo's free version is fully functional for learning — you just deal with ads and limited hearts. Canceling Super (formerly Plus) doesn't affect your streak, XP, or progress.

Open subscription settings

On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Duolingo. On Android: Google Play → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Duolingo. On web: duolingo.com/settings/account.

Duolingo Super is almost always billed through Apple or Google, not Duolingo directly. Check your app store first.

Find your Duolingo subscription

Locate Duolingo in your active subscriptions list. Tap on it to see plan details and renewal date.

Tap "Cancel Subscription"

Tap "Cancel Subscription" or "Cancel free trial". Confirm when prompted.

If you're on a free trial, cancel before it ends to avoid the first charge. You keep the trial until the expiry date.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Most Duolingo subscriptions are through Apple/Google — you must cancel through their subscription settings, not the Duolingo app.
  • Free Duolingo uses a 'hearts' system — you lose hearts for wrong answers and must wait or practice to regain them.
  • Offline lessons are disabled on the free plan. Download lessons won't work after cancellation.
  • Your streak, progress, and league position are completely preserved.

✓ Refund may be possible

Since most subscriptions are through Apple/Google, request refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play. Duolingo direct subscriptions can be refunded by contacting their support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my streak if I cancel Duolingo Super?
No. Your streak, XP, leagues, achievements, and all learning progress are completely preserved on the free plan. Only premium perks (no ads, unlimited hearts) are removed.
Can I still learn on free Duolingo?
Absolutely. Free Duolingo gives you access to all courses and lessons. You'll see ads between lessons and have limited hearts (5 mistakes before waiting), but the learning content is identical.
Can I get a refund from Duolingo?
Request refunds through Apple App Store (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (play.google.com/store/account) since most subscriptions are billed through them.
What's the hearts system on free Duolingo?
You start with 5 hearts. Each wrong answer costs 1 heart. When hearts run out, you wait a few hours to refill or do practice sessions to earn them back. Super removes this limit entirely.
