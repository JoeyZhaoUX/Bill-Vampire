How to Cancel iCloud+ — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2-3 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $2.99/mo 🛡️ Written & Verified by Consumer Advocacy Team

Canceling iCloud+ means downgrading to the free 5GB storage plan. If you're using more than 5GB, your data isn't deleted — but iCloud stops syncing new data until you free up space.

Open Apple ID subscription settings

iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Change Storage Plan. Mac: System Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage → Change Storage Plan.

Select "Downgrade Options"

At the bottom of storage plans, tap "Downgrade Options". Enter your Apple ID password when prompted.

Select the free 5GB plan

Choose the 5GB (free) option from the list. Apple will warn you if your current usage exceeds 5GB.

Check what's using your iCloud storage first: Photos, backups, and Drive are usually the biggest consumers.

Confirm downgrade

Tap "Done" to confirm. The downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then, you still have your full storage.

Move photos to Google Photos (15GB free) or download backups locally before the downgrade takes effect.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you're over 5GB, iCloud stops syncing new data (photos, backups, documents). Existing data isn't deleted immediately.
  • Device backups will fail if you're over the 5GB limit. Your iPhone may nag you daily.
  • iCloud Photos library won't sync new photos until you're under the limit.
  • Apple may delete data after an extended period if you remain significantly over the limit (180+ days).
  • iCloud Private Relay, Hide My Email, and custom email domains (iCloud+ features) are disabled.

✓ Refund may be possible

Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple is generally accommodating for recent iCloud charges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Apple delete my photos if I downgrade iCloud?
Not immediately. Apple keeps your data for 180+ days over the limit. But iCloud stops syncing new content. Download photos and backups locally before downgrading.
What uses the most iCloud storage?
Usually: Photos (often 50-100GB+), Device Backups (5-10GB each), iCloud Drive documents. Check in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.
Can I get a refund for iCloud+?
Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple typically accommodates refund requests for recent charges.
What's a free alternative to iCloud for photos?
Google Photos (15GB free, with compression), Amazon Photos (unlimited if you have Prime), and local backup to a computer or external drive.
