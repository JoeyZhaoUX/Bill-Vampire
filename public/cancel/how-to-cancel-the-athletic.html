How to Cancel The Athletic Without Missing a Separate Charge (2026)

⏰ 4 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-08-25 Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

The Athletic can appear inside a New York Times bundle and still have a separate standalone subscription. Before cancelling anything, open the receipt and identify whether NYTimes, Apple, or Google Play processed the payment.

Official account or cancellation path The Athletic

Match the charge to its billing channel

Check the receipt and the Subscription Overview in your New York Times account. The overview should direct you to the place that manages the subscription.

Look for a standalone Athletic plan even if your current NYT plan already includes Athletic access. They can be separate purchases.

Cancel a standalone Athletic subscription

Open nytimes.com/athletic/settings while signed in to the paying account. Follow the cancellation option and review the date on which paid access ends.

The New York Times terms also allow Athletic subscribers to contact customer support when the account page does not resolve the charge.

Manage an NYT bundle in the NYT account

If The Athletic is included in All Access or Home Delivery, use the New York Times account area to manage the bundle. Cancelling an NYT subscription does not automatically cancel a separate standalone Athletic plan.

If you upgraded from standalone Athletic to All Access, cancel the old standalone plan separately after confirming the bundle is active.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

Use Apple or Google Play for an app-store purchase

A subscription purchased through iOS or Google Play must be cancelled with that third party. Save the store confirmation and check that the next renewal is gone.

Refund requests also go to the provider that processed the payment.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Cancelling a New York Times subscription does not automatically cancel a separately billed Athletic subscription.
  • Paid cancellations normally take effect at the end of the current subscription period. A cancellation during a free trial takes effect immediately under the current NYT terms.
  • Third-party purchases must be changed or cancelled through the same third party.
  • Old instructions that point only to theathletic.com/account may miss the current NYTimes account route.

✓ Refund may be possible

A refund is not automatic. Use the provider on the receipt and include the payment date, cancellation confirmation, and any evidence of a duplicate or unexpected charge. Local consumer rights may add protections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Athletic included with my New York Times plan?
The current NYT help page says All Access and Home Delivery include The Athletic. Check the What's included section in your Subscription Overview because plan availability can vary.
Why do I still see an Athletic charge after changing my NYT plan?
A standalone Athletic subscription can remain separate from the new bundle. Check the Athletic settings page and the merchant name on the receipt for a second renewal.
Can I get a refund from The Athletic?
Refund rights depend on the charge, billing provider, location, and timing. Contact NYT customer support for a direct charge. Apple or Google handles requests for purchases made through its store.
What happens to access after cancellation?
For a normal paid subscription, current NYT terms say access continues through the remainder of the subscription period. After that, subscriber-only coverage requires another eligible plan.
