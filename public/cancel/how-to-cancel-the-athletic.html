Some NYT All Access plans include The Athletic. Check your NYT account benefits before paying for a separate Athletic subscription.
Can I get a refund from The Athletic?
Contact The Athletic support for refund requests. They may offer prorated refunds for annual plans. Apple/Google subscriptions must go through those platforms.

You lose access to all articles and podcasts when your billing period ends. No free tier is available.
