How to Cancel ExpressVPN — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 3-5 minutes medium Verified 2026-05-22 $12.95/mo

ExpressVPN offers a 30-day money-back guarantee — if you're within that window, you get a full refund. After 30 days, you can still turn off auto-renewal to prevent future charges.

Sign in to your ExpressVPN account

Visit expressvpn.com/subscriptions and sign in with your credentials.

Turn off auto-renewal

In your subscription settings, find the auto-renewal toggle and turn it off. This prevents future charges when your current plan expires.

Request a refund if within 30 days

If you're within 30 days of purchase: contact ExpressVPN support via live chat. Tell them you want to cancel and request a refund under the money-back guarantee.

Live chat is the fastest method. Be direct: 'I'd like to cancel and request a refund under your 30-day guarantee.'

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund from ExpressVPN?
Yes, within 30 days of purchase under their money-back guarantee. Contact live chat support. After 30 days, no refund is available.
How long does an ExpressVPN refund take?
Typically 5-7 business days for credit cards. PayPal may be faster (2-3 days). Bitcoin refunds are processed in the original cryptocurrency.
Will my ExpressVPN account be deleted?
After cancellation, your account remains but without active VPN service. You can reactivate anytime with a new subscription.
