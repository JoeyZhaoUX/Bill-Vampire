How to Cancel Dropbox — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 3-5 minutes medium Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $11.99/mo 🛡️ Written & Verified by Consumer Advocacy Team

Dropbox cancellation downgrades you to the free Basic plan (2GB). If you're over 2GB, your files aren't deleted — but you can't sync new files until you're under the limit. Annual plan users face an early termination fee.

Go to Dropbox account settings

Visit dropbox.com/account/plan while signed in to your Dropbox account.

Click "Cancel plan" or "Downgrade"

Find your current plan (Plus, Professional, or Family) and click "Cancel plan" or "Downgrade to Basic". Dropbox will show what changes.

Review the impact and early termination fee

If you're on an annual plan, Dropbox may charge an early cancellation fee. Review the fee and your options. If near the end of your term, it may be cheaper to wait.

If you're paying monthly, there's no early termination fee — just cancel and it ends at the next billing date.

Confirm cancellation

Select your reason, decline any offers, and confirm. Your paid features remain active until the end of your billing period. After that, you switch to Basic (2GB).

Before canceling: download any files you want to keep locally. If you're over 2GB on the free plan, Dropbox stops syncing but doesn't delete your cloud files.

⚠️ Watch out

  • Annual plans may incur an early termination fee if canceled before the term ends.
  • If your stored files exceed 2GB, Dropbox stops syncing but doesn't delete files. You need to reduce usage or files become view-only.
  • Shared folders may stop syncing for collaborators if you were the only paid user.
  • Smart Sync and advanced features are disabled on Basic. Files set to 'online-only' remain in the cloud.
  • Dropbox Passwords, Vault, and extended version history (180 days → 30 days) are lost.

✓ Refund may be possible

Dropbox offers full refunds within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans may incur an early termination fee. Monthly plans are non-refundable but have no termination fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my files be deleted if I cancel Dropbox?
No. Files are never deleted by Dropbox when you downgrade. If over 2GB, your account becomes read-only (can't add new files) but existing files stay in the cloud.
What's Dropbox's early termination fee?
For annual plans canceled early, Dropbox may charge a fee equivalent to the remaining months (pro-rated). Monthly plans have no early termination fee.
Can I get a refund from Dropbox?
Dropbox offers refunds within 30 days of initial purchase. After that, annual plans have early termination fees. Contact Dropbox support for refund requests.
What's a free alternative to Dropbox?
Google Drive (15GB free), iCloud (5GB free), and OneDrive (5GB free) are the main alternatives. Sync.com offers 5GB with better privacy.
