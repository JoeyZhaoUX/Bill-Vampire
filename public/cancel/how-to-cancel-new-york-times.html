How to Cancel New York Times — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 10-20 minutes hard Verified 2026-05-22 $17/mo

The New York Times is one of the hardest subscriptions to cancel. Some plans require a phone call or live chat — you can't always do it online. They're also aggressive with retention offers. Here's how to get through it.

Try online cancellation first

Go to myaccount.nytimes.com and sign in. Navigate to Subscription overview. Look for a "Cancel" option. Some newer subscriptions allow online cancellation.

If subscribed through Apple/Google, skip to their subscription settings instead.

If no online option: use live chat

Click "Contact Us" or "Help" at the bottom of your account page. Start a live chat and tell them you want to cancel. Be direct and firm.

Say: 'I want to cancel my subscription effective immediately. Please process this now.' Don't engage with retention offers if you're sure.

If chat doesn't work: call them

Call NYT customer service at 1-800-NYTIMES (1-800-698-4637). Weekday mornings have shortest wait times. Tell the agent you want to cancel.

Calling is the nuclear option but it always works. Average hold time is 5-10 minutes.

Get written confirmation

Ask for email confirmation of your cancellation. Check your inbox. If you don't receive one within 24 hours, check your account to verify the subscription shows as canceled.

Screenshot the confirmation. NYT has been known to 'fail to process' cancellations.

⚠️ Watch out

  • NYT is notorious for making cancellation difficult. Online cancellation isn't always available — you may need to call or chat.
  • Agents will aggressively offer discounts (often $4/mo or $1/week). Decline firmly if you want to cancel.
  • If subscribed through Apple/Google, cancel through their platforms instead — much easier.
  • NYT occasionally 'loses' cancellation requests. Always get written confirmation.
  • Games-only and Cooking-only subscriptions may have different cancellation paths than the full digital subscription.

✓ Refund may be possible

NYT may offer prorated refunds, especially for annual subscriptions. Ask during your cancellation call. For Apple/Google subscriptions, request refunds through those platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I cancel NYT online?
NYT deliberately makes online cancellation unavailable for many plan types, forcing you to call or chat with a retention agent. This is a dark pattern to reduce cancellations.
Can I get a refund from the New York Times?
NYT occasionally offers prorated refunds, especially for annual plans. Ask the agent during your cancellation call. They're more likely to give refunds than let you cancel outright.
What's the best time to call NYT to cancel?
Weekday mornings (9-10 AM ET) have the shortest wait times. Avoid Mondays and lunch hours. Weekend support is limited.
Will NYT offer me a discount if I try to cancel?
Almost certainly yes. NYT agents are trained to offer $4/month, $1/week, or similar deals. If you genuinely want to cancel, say 'No thank you, please process the cancellation' firmly.
