How to Cancel Claude Pro — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 1 minute easy Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $20/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Anthropic makes Claude Pro cancellation simple — three clicks and you're done. You keep access to Claude Free with lower usage limits, and all your conversation history is preserved.

Official account or cancellation path Claude Pro

Open Claude billing settings

Go to claude.ai/settings/billing while signed in to your Claude account. You can also access this by clicking your name in the bottom-left → Settings → Billing.

Click "Cancel plan" or "Manage subscription"

Find the cancellation option in your billing settings. Click "Cancel plan" or "Manage subscription" depending on the current UI.

Confirm cancellation

Confirm your cancellation. Your Pro features (higher usage limits, priority access, early feature access) remain active until the end of your current billing period.

You keep all conversation history and Projects on the free plan. The main difference is reduced message limits.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you subscribed through the iOS app (Apple), cancel through Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.
  • Claude Free has significantly lower daily message limits compared to Pro.
  • Your conversation history, Projects, and custom instructions are all preserved on the free plan.
  • If you're on a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, ask your workspace admin to manage the subscription.

✓ Refund may be possible

Contact support@anthropic.com for refund requests. Anthropic handles refunds on a case-by-case basis. Recent charges from accidental renewals are typically refunded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my conversation history if I cancel Claude Pro?
No. All conversations, Projects, and custom instructions are preserved on the free plan. You just have lower usage limits.
What's the difference between Claude Free and Pro?
Pro gives you 5x more usage, priority access during peak times, and early access to new features and models. Free has the same models but with daily message limits.
Can I get a refund for Claude Pro?
Contact Anthropic support at support@anthropic.com for refund requests. They handle these on a case-by-case basis.
How do I cancel Claude Pro on iPhone?
If you subscribed via the iOS app: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Claude → Cancel. If you subscribed on the web, cancel at claude.ai/settings/billing.
