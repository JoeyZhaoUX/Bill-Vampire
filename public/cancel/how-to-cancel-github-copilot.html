How to Cancel GitHub Copilot — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 1 minute easy Last Updated: 2026-05-22 $10/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

GitHub Copilot Individual can be canceled directly from your GitHub settings. The free tier now includes limited completions, so you don't lose AI assistance entirely.

Official account or cancellation path: GitHub Copilot

Go to GitHub Copilot settings

Visit github.com/settings/copilot while signed in to your GitHub account. Or: click your avatar → Settings → Copilot.

Click "Cancel" or "Manage subscription"

In the Copilot settings page, find your subscription details and click "Cancel" or navigate to the billing section.

If Copilot was provided by your organization, you cannot cancel it yourself — contact your org admin.

Confirm cancellation

Select a reason for canceling and confirm. Your Copilot access continues until the end of your current billing period.

GitHub Copilot Free gives you limited completions per month — it may be enough if you're not coding full-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GitHub Copilot free for students?
Yes. Verified students and teachers get GitHub Copilot free through GitHub Education (education.github.com). If you're eligible, you don't need a paid subscription.
What's the difference between Copilot Free and Pro?
Free gives you limited completions per month. Pro ($10/mo) gives unlimited completions, chat, and access to multiple models. Business ($19/mo) adds org management and policy controls.
Can I get a refund for GitHub Copilot?
GitHub handles refunds on a case-by-case basis. Contact GitHub support through their help portal for refund requests.
What are good free alternatives to GitHub Copilot?
Codeium (free AI completions), Cody by Sourcegraph (free tier), and Amazon CodeWhisperer (free for individual use) are solid alternatives.
