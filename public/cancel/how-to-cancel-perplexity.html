How to Cancel Perplexity Pro on Web, iPhone, or Android (2026)

⏰ 1 minute easy Last Updated: 2026-06-28 $20/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Cancel Perplexity Pro through the same channel that billed you: Perplexity settings for web purchases, Apple Subscriptions for iPhone or iPad, or Google Play for Android. The free account currently includes five Pro Searches per day.

Official account or cancellation path Perplexity Pro

Go to Perplexity settings

Visit perplexity.ai/settings/subscription while signed in. Or click your profile icon → Settings → Subscription.

Click "Manage Subscription" or "Cancel"

Find the subscription management section and click "Manage Subscription" or "Cancel Plan".

Confirm cancellation

Click through any confirmation dialogs. Your Pro features (unlimited Pro searches, file upload, API credits) remain active until the end of your billing period.

The free tier still gives you 5 Pro searches per day — often enough for casual use.

Already charged? Keep the receipt and the cancellation confirmation. They are the useful part of a refund request.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If Apple billed you, cancel through iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. If Google billed you, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Free tier limits you to 5 Pro searches per day (vs unlimited on Pro).
  • Your search history, Collections, and saved threads are preserved on the free plan.
  • API access is revoked after cancellation unless you have separate API credits.

✓ Refund may be possible

Contact Perplexity support for refund requests. They typically handle recent charges on a case-by-case basis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I lose when canceling Perplexity Pro?
You lose unlimited Pro searches (drops to 5/day), file/image upload for analysis, higher model access, and API credits. Basic search still works fine.
Can I get a refund from Perplexity?
Contact Perplexity support for refund requests. They handle these case-by-case. Apple/Google subscriptions must be refunded through those platforms.
Is Perplexity Free still useful?
Yes. Free Perplexity gives you unlimited basic searches and 5 Pro searches per day. For most casual research needs, the free tier works well.
Will I lose my search history?
No. All your threads, Collections, and search history are preserved on the free tier.
