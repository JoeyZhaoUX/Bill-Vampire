How to Cancel Disney+ — Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

⏰ 2-3 minutes easy Last Updated: 2026-05-21 $13.99/mo Reviewed by Bill Vampire Editorial Team

Disney+ is relatively straightforward to cancel — no phone calls required. You can do it entirely through your account settings on the web or mobile app.

Official account or cancellation path: Disney+

Log in to Disney+

Go to disneyplus.com and sign in, or open the Disney+ app. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner.

Go to Account settings

Select "Account" from the profile dropdown menu. This takes you to your subscription and billing details.

Click on your subscription

Under the "Subscription" section, click on your current plan (Disney+ Basic, Premium, or Bundle). You'll see your billing details and next renewal date.

If you see 'Billed through Apple' or 'Billed through Google', you'll need to cancel through their respective subscription settings.

Select "Cancel Subscription"

Click "Cancel Subscription" at the bottom of the plan details page. Disney+ will show you what you'll lose and offer alternatives.

Confirm your cancellation

Click "Complete Cancellation" on the final confirmation screen. You'll maintain access until the end of your current billing period.

Note your renewal date — you have access until then and can change your mind anytime before it expires.

⚠️ Watch out

  • If you have the Disney Bundle (Disney+, Hulu, ESPN+), canceling Disney+ may cancel your entire bundle. Check if you can downgrade to individual plans instead.
  • If you subscribed through Apple, Google, Roku, or Amazon, you must cancel through that platform's subscription settings — not on disneyplus.com.
  • Downloads and watch history are preserved for a period after cancellation, but profiles may be deleted after extended inactivity.
  • Promotional pricing (like $1.99/mo offers) will not be available if you re-subscribe later.

✗ Refund policy

Disney+ does not offer prorated refunds. Your access continues until the end of your billing cycle. For billing errors, contact the Disney+ Help Center directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a refund from Disney+?
Disney+ does not typically offer refunds for partial billing periods. However, if you were charged incorrectly or experienced significant service issues, contact Disney+ Help Center for a possible credit.
What happens to my downloads after canceling Disney+?
All downloaded content becomes inaccessible once your subscription expires. Download and watch any saved content before your last day of access.
Can I cancel Disney+ but keep Hulu?
Yes, if you have the Disney Bundle, you can switch to standalone Hulu. Go to Account → Subscription → Switch to Hulu Only. You'll keep your Hulu profile and history.
Will I lose my profiles and watchlist?
Disney+ retains your profile data, watchlist, and viewing history for a reasonable period. If you re-subscribe within a few months, everything should be intact.
