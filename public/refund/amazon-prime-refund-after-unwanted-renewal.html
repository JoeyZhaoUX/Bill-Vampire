What people are running into

Research signal

Refund window

Act immediately after the charge. If you did not use Prime benefits after the renewal, say that clearly and ask support to reverse the most recent membership charge.

Cancel path

Open Amazon account settings, go to Prime Membership, manage membership, cancel or end membership, then save any cancellation confirmation screen or email.

Evidence checklist

  • Prime renewal receipt
  • Cancellation confirmation if you have it
  • Date you attempted to cancel
  • Whether Prime benefits were used after renewal
  • Support chat or call notes
