Amazon Prime refund FAQ

Can I get a refund from Amazon Prime?

Act immediately after the charge. If you did not use Prime benefits after the renewal, say that clearly and ask support to reverse the most recent membership charge.

How do I cancel Amazon Prime?

Open Amazon account settings, go to Prime Membership, manage membership, cancel or end membership, then save any cancellation confirmation screen or email.

What proof should I keep for Amazon Prime?

Keep prime renewal receipt, cancellation confirmation if you have it, date you attempted to cancel, whether prime benefits were used after renewal, and any support replies before escalating.
