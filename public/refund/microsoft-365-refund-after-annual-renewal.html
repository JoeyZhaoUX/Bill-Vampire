Refund window

Cancel through Microsoft account services first, then check whether the flow offers a refund. If not, contact support with the order number, charge date, and account email.

Cancel path

Open Microsoft account Services & subscriptions, find Microsoft 365, choose Manage, then Cancel subscription and follow the refund eligibility prompts.

Evidence checklist

  • Microsoft order number
  • Renewal receipt
  • Account email
  • Cancellation confirmation
  • Support chat transcript
