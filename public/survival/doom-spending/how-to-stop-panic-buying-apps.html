How to stop panic buying apps

Set a cooling-off workflow for trials, app purchases, and recurring plans. Bill Vampire turns the pressure into a focused subscription survival preview without a bank login.

What to inspect first

  • late-night app purchases
  • free trials started during stress
  • wellness or productivity tools bought impulsively
  • annual plans bought from ads
  • subscriptions that promised relief but went unused

Checklist

  • List purchases from the last 30 days.
  • Mark which ones were used, unused, or bought during stress.
  • Cancel future renewals first.
  • Ask for refunds on recent unused charges.
  • Set a cooling-off reminder before starting new trials.
