What's happening

Heading into 2026, streaming prices climbed again and ad-free tiers got pricier across Netflix, Disney+, and Max, while password-sharing crackdowns ended the old workarounds. People are deciding what to cut first now that sharing one account across households isn't an option. The question for 2026 is which services earn their place when each one wants its own full subscription.

Your first move in the next 10 minutes

Write down every streaming bill and the date of the last thing you finished watching on each. Any service where that date is more than two months ago is a clear cancel. Do it before the next renewal so you stop paying for catalogs you've stopped opening.

What to cut or check first

  • Cancel the service you haven't opened in 60 days, no matter how good the catalog looks
  • Drop services you only kept for password sharing now that it's blocked
  • Cut to one premium streamer and use free ad-supported apps for everything else
  • Cancel anything you signed up for in 2025 for a single limited series
  • Drop the ad-free upgrade and accept ads on your secondary service
  • Pause sports or seasonal streamers during the off-season months

Hi, I want to cancel my [streaming service] subscription for 2026 effective [date]. With prices rising I'm consolidating to fewer services. Please confirm the cancellation and that I won't be billed again. If you can match a lower price or offer a pause, I'm open to hearing it first.

What to keep an eye on

Keep the one streamer carrying the live sports or annual event you won't miss, and any you prepaid through 2026. Watch the new ad-supported tiers, sometimes the ad version of a service you love is cheaper than canceling and re-adding it later.

FAQ

Which streaming service should I cancel first in 2026?

The one you've opened least in the last two months. Recency of use beats how impressive a catalog looks. If you can't remember the last thing you watched there, that's your first cancellation.

Now that password sharing is blocked, is it cheaper to share or rotate?

Rotating your own subscriptions month to month is now the cleaner move. With sharing cracked down on across the major services, paying for one streamer at a time and cycling beats juggling extra-member fees.

Should I switch to ad-supported streaming tiers in 2026?

If the price gap is more than a few dollars and you don't mind ad breaks, yes. The ad tiers got cheaper relative to ad-free this year, and for casual watching the savings usually outweigh the interruptions.
