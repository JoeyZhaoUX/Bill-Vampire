How we review cancellation and refund guidance.

Bill Vampire is an independent subscription-action tool. We are not a bank, law firm, card network, or representative of the services covered in our guides.

Source order

  1. Official account, billing, cancellation, and support pages.
  2. Official app-store billing and refund documentation.
  3. Community discussions used only to identify recurring user problems—not as policy.

What “reviewed” means

We check the destination of the official account or cancellation link, remove obsolete plan numbers when the provider no longer publishes them, separate web billing from Apple or Google billing, and date the review. Providers can change their interfaces or policies without notice.

AI-assisted drafting

Automation may help structure drafts and communication templates. Published guidance is reviewed against the linked source. AI-generated scripts are starting points for contacting support and do not guarantee cancellation, refunds, disputes, or chargeback outcomes.

Corrections

If a cancel path, price, refund condition, or source is outdated, email hello@billvampire.com. Include the affected URL and an official source when possible.
